Read this before you quote it

Outcome labels are assigned by rules that read the docket text. They are a sorting aid, and each bill's detail shows the exact docket line its label came from. The docket, the bill page, and the roll-call record on gc.nh.gov are the record. Every bill here links to them.

  • The General Court is not in session. Work still under way means interim-study bills with committee sessions scheduled this fall and laws with effective dates still ahead. The 2027 filings are not yet in the state's data.
  • "Still in process" means the docket file shows no final action. For many of these bills, the General Court's bill table records a later floor action that the docket file does not include. Each one says so, and links to the official record. Check it before calling a bill pending.
  • A veto overridden in one chamber and sustained in the other stands. The tracker counts it as sustained.
  • "Not voting, not excused" is the General Court's own label. A member marked absent on every vote may have resigned or the seat may have been vacant; check before naming anyone.
  • Subject and county labels are inferred from the raw codes and shown beside them. The prior-session file is docket-only, with no titles or sponsors.
  • LegiScan, Plural, and news-search links are built from the bill number and not checked bill by bill.

How it is made

A script pulls the pipe-delimited files the General Court publishes at gc.nh.gov/downloads, once a night in the hours the General Court asks automated clients to use, and rebuilds the dataset. The code, the data, and a column-by-column audit are public on GitHub. Read the method and caveats or download the data as JSON. Corrections go to the Corrections Register.
