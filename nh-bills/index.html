    Source. The New Hampshire General Court publishes its bill-status database as text files at gc.nh.gov/downloads. This page reads a copy refreshed daily by an automated job, early in the morning Eastern, when the General Court asks automated clients to use the site.

    Read this before you quote it. Outcome labels are assigned by rules that read the docket text. They are a sorting aid. The docket, the bill page, and the roll-call record on gc.nh.gov are the record. Every bill here links to them.

    Links. LegiScan, Plural, and news-search links are built from the bill number, not checked bill by bill. A search may return nothing for a bill nobody has written about.

    Subjects and counties. The General Court's subject codes and county codes come without a published key. The labels here were inferred from the bills filed under each code and are shown next to the raw code. Legislator party comes from the roster file.
