      Source. The New Hampshire General Court publishes its bill-status database as text files at gc.nh.gov/downloads. This page reads a copy refreshed nightly, in the early-morning hours the General Court asks automated clients to use.

      Read this before you quote it. Outcome labels are assigned by rules that read the docket text. They are a sorting aid. The docket, the bill page, and the roll-call record on gc.nh.gov are the record, and every bill here links to them.

      Still in process. The docket file shows no final action for these bills. For many of them, the General Court's bill table records a later floor action the docket file does not include. Check the official record before calling a bill pending.

      Labels. Subject and county labels are inferred from the General Court's unpublished codes and shown beside them. LegiScan, Plural, and news-search links are built from the bill number and not checked bill by bill.
