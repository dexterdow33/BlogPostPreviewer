How this room works Free, and it stays free. The records themselves belong to the public: every file listed here sits at a public address on this site, and anyone can read one. What this newsroom made is the index, and the index is open too. Each entry names where the document came from, when, and which Granite State Report articles rest on it. Submit a record

New Hampshire keeps its records in ten thousand filing cabinets, and most of them never see daylight. If you have one, this is where it can live: a police report, a town or city record, a state agency letter, a court filing, correspondence with a public office, a response to your own Right-to-Know request. Anything a government body produced or sent you.

Send the document with the form below. Say where it came from and how you got it. That is the whole ask.
