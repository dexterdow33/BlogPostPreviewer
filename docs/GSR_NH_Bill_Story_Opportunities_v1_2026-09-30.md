## First, the premise

The General Court is not in session. The House docket marks tabled bills "Died on Table, Session ended 08/19/2026," and the last floor votes were the veto-day roll calls on August 19. So "bills in progress" today means three things, and only three:

1. **215 bills in interim study**, of which **63 have committee sessions on the calendar between September 30 and October 23.** That is the live legislative work in New Hampshire right now.
2. **Laws with effective dates still ahead**: 14 between October 1 and December 31, and **59 on January 1, 2027.**
3. **The 2027 filing pipeline.** Incumbents file legislative service requests for 2027 in September; published guides for the 2026 cycle gave September 1 to 11 for the House, with filing reopening after the November election. **VERIFY** this year's dates with the House Clerk. Those LSRs are **not** in the state's data dump, which lists only the current session. They should appear in the LSR search at gc.nh.gov/lsr_search, which I could not reach from this environment.

At least one published summary says the Senate "voted to override" SB 434. True, and the veto stood anyway. That is a correction-shaped story.

Other veto facts in the docket:

- **Closest failed override**: HB 1072 (employer notice of Department of Labor investigations), House 160-159, short of two-thirds. Republicans 160-15, Democrats 0-144.
- **The Senate voted 0-24 against overriding three of its own bills**: SB 268 (biological sex classification), SB 481 (sale of the Sununu Youth Services Center property), SB 627 (turnpike toll rate adjustments).
- **Tolls**: SB 627 was vetoed and sustained 0-24 while HB 2026, the ten-year transportation plan that also carries toll adjustments, was overridden 292-25 and 24-0 and enacted as Chapter 343 without the governor's signature. Section 4 takes effect Dec. 1, 2026; sections 3 and 5 on Jan. 1, 2027. **VERIFY** which section is the toll change by reading the chaptered text.
- **All three biological-sex bills were vetoed and all three vetoes stood**: HB 1442 (165-149), SB 268 (0-24), SB 552 (split above).
- **Count conflict**: the docket shows 32 vetoed bills in the 2026 file; the Concord Monitor reported 32 and NHPR's veto-day story said 33. Twelve more vetoes sit in the 2025 file, all sustained, for 44 this biennium. Say "32 in the 2026 file" and cite the docket.

## Where the data can mislead you

- Outcome labels are rule-based readings of docket text. The drawer shows the "decisive action" line each label came from. Read it.
- "Not voting" totals can reflect a vacancy or resignation, not truancy.
- Subject labels and county names are inferred; the raw codes are shown beside them.
- The 2025 file is docket-only: bill numbers and actions, no titles or sponsors.
- LegiScan, Plural, and news-search links are built from the bill number and not checked bill by bill.
- Vote tallies quoted above are from RollCallSummary.txt; journal pages cited in the docket are the source of record.
